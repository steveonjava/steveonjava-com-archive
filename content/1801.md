I was fortunate enough to be invited to speak at the [Chennai Java User Group](http://www.jugchennai.in/2011/02/20/javafx-with-stephen-chin-java-user-group-chennai-at-first-planet-19-February/) during my trip to India.  I was expecting a small group of very devoted Java fans; however, I was surprised to walk into a room of over 200 developers eager to learn about JavaFX 2.0.  The venue was very impressive with rows of workstations that we later used for a lab, as well as plenty of seating.

[![Chennai JUG](/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/DSC_0035-650x435.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/DSC_0035.jpg)

*Chennai User Group Venue at Tenth Planet*

Also, [Raj](http://twitter.com/rajmahendra) was a great host, and both he and the folks at [Tenth Planet](http://tenthplanet.in/) went completely over the top with this event.  This included:

- A life-size poster with the event details
- An ornamental flower arrangement with the event details
- Two gifts presented at the conclusion of the talk

[![Chennai JUG Poster](/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/DSC_0037-650x435.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2011/02/DSC_0037.jpg)

*Life-Size Event Poster*

The session ran from 10AM through 4:30PM with a break for lunch in the middle.  When half the hands went up for returning after lunch, I didn't believe most of them would be back, but we easily had 60% of the folks back in their seats by the time we were ready to start.

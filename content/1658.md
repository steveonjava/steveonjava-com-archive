For those of you who haven't been following the Flash Mobile headlines, there have been a lot of great announcements that make this platform worth developing for:

**Mobile**

- With the [AIR 2.5 release](http://get.adobe.com/air/), Android devices are fully supported
- Apple has [relaxed their license](http://www.apple.com/pr/library/2010/09/09statement.html) to allow Flash-based applications in the App Store
- Similar announcements have come from other vendors such as [Palm](http://pressroom.palm.com/releasedetail.cfm?ReleaseID=519951), [Windows 7, and others](http://www.engadget.com/2010/10/25/adobe-confirms-flash-player-10-1-is-coming-to-blackberry-window/)

**TV**

- [Google TV](http://www.google.com/tv/features.html) prominently features Flash support
- Adobe also announced [AIR support for Samsung devices](http://eon.businesswire.com/news/eon/20101024005146/en/Adobe-Extends-AIR-Applications-Screens) such as Smart TVs and Blu-ray Players

**Tablet**

- Blackberry announced [Adobe AIR support](http://us.blackberry.com/newsroom/news/press/release.jsp?id=4674) for their Playbook Tablet

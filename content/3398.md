I have been involved in the Geek Bike Ride events from the inception in Brazil, and I have to say that the event yesterday in Shanghai was the hottest ever\! Well, literally the hottest, because of the 100 degree weather (plus humidity), but also with a great turnout from the local [GreenTea JUG](http://www.headin.cn/Themes/Activity/Details?activityId=51dbe2d4869d081fd43ed4d4).
